FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I monetize my weather insights?

While Marco collects weather data from your location, you’re contributing to the broader Nubila Oracle network. Contributions are periodically rewarded in $NB tokens that can be tracked directly in the mobile app or user dashboard. No locking, no subscriptions — simply run your station and earn.

Do I need to charge or replace batteries?

Nope. Marco includes a solar panel so it can run day and night with zero external power during normal use. The 4000mAh internal battery keeps the station running for 3+ days without direct sun and automatically recharges when sunlight returns.

What makes Marco different from other weather stations?

All weather stations give you data but only Marco gives you data and tangible rewards. Every measurement becomes a verified oracle signal used by AI models, DeFi protocols, and enterprises — with value redistributed back to contributors like you.

Is it difficult to install?

Not at all. Most users set up Marco in under 10 minutes — no technicians. Just mount it on a pole, fence, or rooftop with a clear view of the sky, download the Nubila app, and follow the on-screen pairing flow over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi.

What kind of weather does Marco track?

Marco continuously tracks temperature, humidity, UV, wind, and air pressure — with sensor-level accuracy documented in the technical specifications. Data is displayed in real time inside the Nubila mobile app.

What happens if it rains, snows, or the weather gets rough?

Marco is built to last. With IP56 protection, it handles rain, wind, dust, and temperatures from -40°C to 80°C. The rugged metal chassis and outdoor-rated plastics mean it’s engineered to sit on your roof for years without maintenance.

Return & Refund Policy

1. Return Eligibility

Hardware products purchased directly from Nubila may be returned within thirty (30) calendar days of the original ship date, provided ALL of the following conditions are met:

  • The device must be in original physical condition
  • The device must not be damaged or modified
  • The device must be fully unbound from any Nubila account prior to return
  • All original accessories must be included

Failure to unbind the device from the associated account will delay or void processing of your refund.

2. Return Authorization

Prior to returning any product, customers must contact Nubila at hi@nubila.ai to request a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA). We will guide you through the return process with the exact return address, packing requirements, and expected timelines.

3. Shipping Responsibility

Customers are responsible for:

  • All return shipping costs
  • Secure packaging of the device

Nubila is not responsible for damage occurring during return shipment. Original outbound shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is a result of our error (wrong item shipped, defective out of the box, etc.).

4. Refund Processing

Upon receipt and inspection of the returned product:

  • If approved, a refund will be issued to the original payment method
  • Refund processing may take 5–10 business days

Refund amount will exclude:

  • Original shipping fees
  • Any applicable transaction fees (if required by payment processor)
5. Non-Returnable Items

The following are not eligible for return:

  • Devices damaged due to misuse
  • Devices modified or opened without authorization
  • Devices not unbound from user accounts
  • Products returned after the 30-day return window
6. Warranty vs. Return

This Return Policy is separate from Nubila’s Limited Hardware Warranty. Warranty claims relating to defects after the 30-day return window are handled through the limited warranty process outlined in the product documentation, not through the standard return flow.
