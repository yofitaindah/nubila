Redeem Marco

Stake & Claim Your Marco

Stake $NBNT in the 180-day vault and redeem a free Marco weather station.

200,000 $NBNT = 1 free Marco 100,000 $NBNT = 50% discount code

How it works

  • Every 200,000 $NBNT earns one more free Marco — 400,000 = 2, 600,000 = 3.
  • Maximum 3 per wallet address.
  • Only the 180-day vault counts. Stakes in the 30-day vault do not qualify.
  • Each code can be used once, and only on the Marco product.
  • Staking is not spending — your $NBNT stays yours and unlocks when the lock period ends.

Check an address

Before you redeem

  • Shipping is not included. Delivery costs are paid by the recipient at checkout.
  • Taxes and customs duties are not included. Orders shipped outside the United States may incur additional fees.
  • Eligibility is read live from the vault contract at the moment you check. Unstaking before you claim will void your code.
  • Codes are issued per wallet address and are not transferable between addresses.
  • Questions? Email gm@nubila.network.
